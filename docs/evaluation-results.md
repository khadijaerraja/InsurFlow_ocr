# Extended evaluation results

Run on 1 November 2026 against the 21 synthetic documents (`data/documents/`), plus 3 extra files (one prompt-injection
invoice, one real photographed receipt, one real photograph that is not a document). PostgreSQL 15 + pgvector, a
throw-away database, real `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` embeddings (FastEmbed), answers generated by Groq `openai/gpt-oss-20b`
and judged by `openai/gpt-oss-120b`. Rule-based extraction only (the LLM extraction fallback was off).

> Scope and caveats: 43 questions and 21 documents are a small, synthetic set generated by the same code that
> produced the documents the thresholds were tuned on, so these numbers are optimistic. The real-document test has
> two files. RAGAS ran on 16 answers. Treat everything as evidence of behaviour, not of production accuracy.

## 2. OCR robustness across formats (21 documents x 7 variants = 147 images/PDFs)

Reference text = native text layer of the clean PDF. CER/WER computed on whitespace-insensitive / lower-cased text.

| Variant (format) | CER mean | CER median | WER mean | Field accuracy (*) | Docs 100 % correct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Scanned PDF (as shipped) | 0.015 | 0.006 | 0.647 | 126/127 = 99.2 % | 20/21 |
| PNG clean, 150 dpi | 0.004 | 0.000 | 0.483 | 99.2 % | 20/21 |
| JPEG quality 30 | 0.007 | 0.004 | 0.545 | 100 % | 21/21 |
| Gaussian noise, JPEG | 0.007 | 0.003 | 0.464 | 100 % | 21/21 |
| Low resolution 80 dpi | 0.018 | 0.003 | 0.291 | 99.2 % | 20/21 |
| Rotated 3 deg + blur, JPEG | **0.264** | 0.268 | 0.668 | **59.8 %** | **0/21** |
| Simulated phone photo (tilt 2.5 deg, uneven light, blur, JPEG 45) | **0.263** | 0.265 | 0.531 | **59.1 %** | **0/21** |

(*) Adjusted: the 2 amendments carry no expiry date, so the expected `end_date` copied from the contract in `ground_truth.json` was
not a valid target; those 2 fields per variant are excluded (129 -> 127 fields). Unadjusted figures were 97.7-98.4 % on the clean
variants and 58.1-58.9 % on the two tilted ones.

Per field (all variants, unadjusted; `end_date` is affected by the note above): doc_type 100 %, reference 100 %, policy_number 97.3 %, amount 89.1 %, document_date 71.4 %,
annual_ceiling 71.4 %, start_date 71.4 %, end_date 53.6 %. By language: CER 0.079 (EN) vs 0.105 (FR).

Diagnostic on 6 documents (isolating the cause of the collapse):

| Condition | CER | Field accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| Clean | 0.002 | 38/39 |
| Blur only (sigma 1.2) | 0.002 | 38/39 |
| **Rotation only (3 deg)** | **0.256** | 21/39 |
| Rotation 3 deg + deskew (projection-profile, OpenCV) | 0.005 | 36/39 |

Cause: `src/ingestion/ocr.py` groups text boxes into lines with a fixed 15 px threshold on the top edge, so a 3 deg tilt
(about 65 px of drift across a page) merges and splits lines, which breaks label/value pairing. Blur and noise are
not the problem. A deskew step before OCR recovers it in this test (not applied to the repository).

Remaining failure on clean scans: one policy number misread by OCR on a claim form (`claim_form_17`, a planted unknown-contract case).

Source: Wikimedia Commons, `TW_Costco_Photo_receipt_20130612.jpg` (JPEG, 2592x1944, 897 KB, a photographed Taiwanese
receipt with Chinese and English text) and `Facture_-_la_gare_4.jpg` (JPEG, 991x620, a 1900s postcard of the village
named "Facture", not an invoice).

| File | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Costco photo receipt | Processed in about 30 s. 10 of 13 Latin/numeric items read (order number `S8748119`, date `102.6.12`, `PHOTO RECEIPT`, `MEMBERSHIP NO.`, `PHONE NO.`, `NAME`, `EMPLOYEE`, `REMARK`, `TIME DUE`, `DATE`); missed the red `COSTCO` logo, read `PHOTO` as `EPHOTO`, and missed the handwritten `17:00`. | Postcard | Caption read (`FACTURE (Gironde) - La Gare`), stamp `25` read. Classified `unknown`, flagged `needs_review`, no fields invented. |

Format support: PDF (native and scanned), PNG and JPEG only. TIFF, WebP, HEIC, DOCX are rejected by the magic-byte check.

## 5. Answer quality

Questions: 27 fact, 11 single-contract semantic, 5 list ("which contracts ..."). Checked by value matching with date
and amount normalisation, not by an LLM.

| Generator | Fully correct | Facts | Single-contract semantic | List questions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Offline extractive (no LLM, no key) | 37/43 = 86.0 % | 27/27 | 10/11 (French question answered with English labels) | 0/5 (cannot aggregate across documents) |
| Groq gpt-oss-20b | 40/43 = 93.0 % | 25/27 | 11/11 | 4/5 fully, 1 partial |

- The 2 Groq fact misses are amendment conflicts: asked for the annual ceiling of INS-001 / INS-003 it returned the
  amended ceiling (100 000 / 1 050 000) instead of the original contract value (50 000 / 1 000 000). Arguably the
  more current value, but the system has no amendment-precedence logic (listed in the README limitations).
- List questions, Groq: precision 1.00, recall 0.95 (it missed INS-006 for "exclude cosmetic surgery").
- Citations (Groq, 43 non-refused answers): 43/43 contain a citation, 43/43 cite a valid excerpt number, and where the
  check applies, 25 of 27 cited excerpts contain the expected value.

## 8. What to fix first (from these results)

1. Deskew before OCR (and group lines by text-box angle, not a fixed 15 px): the single biggest weakness, 59 % field accuracy on tilted pages.
2. Make answers amendment-aware (return the current value and mention the original).
3. Add a handwriting/logo fallback or an explicit "low-confidence region" flag; use the OCR confidence score, which is currently ignored.
4. Let the extractive fallback answer list questions, or route them to the LLM only.
5. Widen the evaluation: more questions, French list questions, real scans, a second embedding model (multilingual), and run it in CI against PostgreSQL.
